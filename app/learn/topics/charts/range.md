The **range** buttons choose how much history to show: 1 month up to *Max* (everything stored).

- **YTD** means *year to date*: from the first trading day of this calendar year.
- The coloured change next to the price (e.g. "+12.3% past year") is measured from the **first close in the
  range** to the latest close, like Google Finance.
- Strategies are backtested over the selected range.

The range is *how far back*; the [candle size](topic:candle-size) is *how detailed*.
