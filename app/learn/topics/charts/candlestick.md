Each **candle** summarises one period (a day, a week...) with four prices, called **OHLC**:

- **Open**: the first trade of the period.
- **High** and **Low**: the highest and lowest prices traded (the thin lines, called *wicks*).
- **Close**: the last trade of the period.

The thick part, the *body*, spans open to close. It is **green** when the price closed **up** (close at or above open)
and **red** when it closed **down**.

## Reading the shapes
- Long bodies show strong buying or selling.
- Long wicks show price was pushed away from an extreme and rejected.
- Many small bodies show indecision.
