**Volume** is the number of shares traded during a candle.

- Bars are **green** when the candle closed up and **red** when it closed down.
- The line is the **average of the last 20 candles**. Bars well above it show unusual interest.
- A big move on **high volume** suggests many traders agree with it. A move on **low volume** is easier to reverse.

The legend shows the latest volume as a multiple of the average, e.g. "1.40x average".
