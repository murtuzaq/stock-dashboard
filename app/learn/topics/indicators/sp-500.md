The **S&P 500** is an index of roughly 500 of the largest companies listed in the US. It is the most common yardstick
for "how the market did", which is why this dashboard uses it as the benchmark for
[relative strength](topic:relative-strength).

An **index** is a calculated number, not something you buy directly; funds called *ETFs* track it so people can invest
in the whole group at once.
