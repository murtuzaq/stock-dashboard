The **signal line** is a 9-period average of the [MACD](topic:macd) line, so it moves more slowly.

- MACD **crosses above** the signal line: momentum is turning up (a common *buy* confirmation).
- MACD **crosses below** the signal line: momentum is turning down (a common *sell* confirmation).

In the strategy builder you can use it as "MACD signal" on either side of a rule.
