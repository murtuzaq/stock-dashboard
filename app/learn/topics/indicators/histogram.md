The **histogram** is the MACD line minus its [signal line](topic:signal-line), drawn as bars around a zero line.

- Bars **above zero**: MACD is above the signal line (momentum rising).
- Bars **below zero**: MACD is below the signal line (momentum falling).
- Bars **growing** show momentum strengthening; bars **shrinking** show it fading, often *before* a cross happens.

The colours follow TradingView: strong when a bar grows, pale when it shrinks.
