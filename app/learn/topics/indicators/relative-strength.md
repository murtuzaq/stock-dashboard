**Relative strength** compares a stock with the market. Here it is the stock's price divided by the
[S&P 500](topic:sp-500), set to **100** at the start of the selected [range](topic:range).

- A line **above 100**: the stock has done better than the market since then.
- A **rising** line: the stock is pulling ahead. A falling one: it is lagging.

The legend also compares the two returns and says by how many percentage points the stock is beating or lagging.

Not to be confused with the **RSI** (Relative Strength *Index*), a different indicator that compares a stock only with
its own recent moves.
