**Overbought** means a price has risen unusually far and fast, so buyers may be exhausted. **Oversold** means it has
fallen unusually far and fast, so sellers may be exhausted.

On a 0-100 oscillator such as [Stoch RSI](topic:stoch-rsi) the usual lines are **80** (overbought) and **20** (oversold).

## Careful
Overbought is **not** a sell signal by itself. In a strong uptrend an indicator can stay overbought for weeks. It is
a warning to look more closely, not an instruction.
