**Revenue** (also called *sales* or the *top line*) is all the money a company earned from selling its products or
services in a period, **before** subtracting costs.

Growing revenue means the business is selling more. It is the first thing to check, but a company can grow revenue and
still lose money, which is why it is read alongside [EPS](topic:eps).
