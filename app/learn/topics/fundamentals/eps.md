**EPS** (earnings per share) is a company's profit divided by its number of shares. This dashboard shows *diluted* EPS,
which counts shares that could be created by options and convertible securities.

Rising EPS means each share earns more. Dividing the share price by EPS gives the **P/E ratio**, a common way to compare
how expensive stocks are.
