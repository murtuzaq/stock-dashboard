- **YoY** (year over year): growth compared with the **same period a year earlier**. Most traders watch this one,
  because it removes the effect of seasons.
- **QoQ** (quarter over quarter): growth compared with the **previous quarter**. Seasonal businesses swing a lot here
  (a retailer's holiday quarter always beats the next one).

## Why a cell shows a dash
A dash means the figure could not be worked out: the year-ago number is not available (the data source provides only
about five quarters) or the earlier figure was a loss, which makes a percentage meaningless.
