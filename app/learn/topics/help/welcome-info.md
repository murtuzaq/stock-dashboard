The **Info Wizard** explains the terms this dashboard shows.

- **Underlined terms** have an explanation. **Double-click** one to read it here.
- Terms inside charts' legends and strategy summaries are underlined too.
- Use the links under *Related topics* to keep exploring, and the arrows above to go back.
- **Don't see an explanation?** Right-click the word and choose *Request info*. Your request is saved so a topic
  can be added.

Turn it off with **View > Info Wizard**; nothing is underlined then.

Everything here is for learning. It is **not investment advice**.
