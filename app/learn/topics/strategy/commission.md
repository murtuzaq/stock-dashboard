A **commission** is the fee a broker charges for placing a trade. Many brokers charge nothing for stocks today, but
some do, and some products have fees.

In a [backtest](topic:backtest) it is a fixed dollar amount charged on **every buy and every sell**, so a strategy
that trades often pays it many times.
