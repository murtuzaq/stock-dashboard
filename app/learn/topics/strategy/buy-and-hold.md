**Buy and hold** means buying at the start and simply keeping the stock. In a backtest it is the **benchmark**: the
dashed line on the equity chart shows what that would have returned over the same range.

A strategy is only worth the effort if it beats it, ideally with a smaller [drawdown](topic:max-drawdown). The summary
shows the difference in percentage points, e.g. "+4.2 pts".
