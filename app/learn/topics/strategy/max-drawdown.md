**Max drawdown** is the largest fall, in percent, from a peak in the account value to the lowest point after it.

If the account grew to $12,000, then fell to $9,000 before recovering, that dip was a 25% drawdown.

## Why it matters
Returns alone hide the risk taken. A strategy that returns 20% but suffered a 45% drawdown is much harder to stick
with than one that returns 15% with a 10% drawdown. The results show it for the strategy and, in brackets, for
[buy and hold](topic:buy-and-hold).
