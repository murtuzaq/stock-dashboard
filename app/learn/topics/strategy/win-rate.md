The **win rate** is the percentage of closed trades that ended with a profit.

## It is not the whole story
- A strategy that wins only **40%** of the time can still be profitable if its wins are much bigger than its losses.
- A strategy that wins **90%** of the time can still lose money if its few losses are huge.

Look at the win rate together with the average win, the average loss and the [max drawdown](topic:max-drawdown).
