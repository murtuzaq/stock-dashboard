A **take-profit** sells automatically when the price rises a set percentage above your entry price, locking in the
gain.

Pairing a take-profit with a [stop-loss](topic:stop-loss) fixes both the best and worst outcome of a trade. The ratio
of the two (for example risking 8% to make 15%) is often called the *risk/reward ratio*.
