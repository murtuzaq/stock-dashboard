An **ATR stop** places the [stop-loss](topic:stop-loss) a multiple of the [ATR](topic:atr) below the entry price, for
example 2 x ATR.

A volatile stock gets a wider stop and a calm one a tighter stop, so normal swings are less likely to knock you out
while real breakdowns still trigger the exit.
